## Indian geography

Map boundaries and the adapted geographic preview are based on geoBoundaries gbOpen IND ADM1, credited by its metadata to DataMeet India community / Election Commission of India. Licensed CC BY 2.5 India.

- Metadata: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/
- Pinned source: https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/geoBoundaries-IND-ADM1_simplified.geojson
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/

Adaptations: normalized region names and polygon ring orientation; simplified/quantized topology and geographic preview; shared-border route graph. Puducherry uses its Tamil Nadu connection only so remote exclaves do not create shortcuts. The preview shows the mainland and the full game includes island territories. These boundaries are used for gameplay, not as an authoritative political or legal statement. No endorsement is implied.

## Atlas Antakshri place data

Derived from GeoNames cities500 and countryInfo (https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/), retrieved 2026-10-25. Licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The dataset selects 40 uniquely named places per initial Latin letter, prioritizing countries, a curated set of familiar cities, capitals, and geographic diversity; city districts are excluded, using ASCII names and retaining GeoNames IDs and country codes. Names are normalized for game matching.
